# WEEKLY KPI tab: rules

*Copied from the "README" tab of the client's Google Sheet. Written by the client.*

## Layout

- Every Monday, add one block of **7 rows** for the week that ended on the previous Saturday,
  below the previous week's block. Use exactly the labels and the order of the template.
- **Do not insert, rename, move or delete columns.** Our dashboards read this tab by column
  position.
- The block must be complete by **Monday 12:00 CET**.

## Calendar

- Our weeks run from **Sunday to Saturday**. Week 1 is the week that contains 1 January.
- Week label: `YYYY-Www`. Example: `2026-W46` = Sunday 8 to Saturday 14 November 2026.

## KPIs

| Column | Definition |
|---|---|
| Visits | Completed store visits by the field team during the week. A visit that covers both product lines counts once in each line. |
| RSAs Trained | Retail sales associates trained, as reported on those visits. Same rule as Visits. |
| Sell-out Units | Units sold during the week, as reported by the retailers. |

`TOTAL` row: every visit counts once (even if it covered both lines). RSAs Trained follows the
same rule. Sell-out Units is the sum of all rows.
